About Runway

Runway is a small set of free tools for people between jobs or on a tight budget.

Losing a job brings up the same questions fast. How long can I last? What should I cut? Where did I apply last week? Most tools that answer them want an account, a bank login or your email address. Runway doesn't.

Our principles

  • Free. Every tool is free. The site is paid for by a few clearly labelled ads.
  • Private. The numbers and applications you enter stay in your browser. We don't have a server that could store them.
  • Honest. The tools do simple, transparent arithmetic. We don't guess at your taxes or benefits, because getting those wrong could hurt you.

Not financial advice

Runway is a planning aid, not a financial, tax or legal advisor. For decisions that matter, check with a qualified professional or the official agency where you live.
