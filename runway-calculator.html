How the calculation works

Your starting balance is savings plus any lump sum. Your net burn is monthly expenses minus monthly income. Runway is simply starting balance ÷ net burn.

Example: $12,000 in savings, $3,000 of expenses and $1,000 of income means you burn $2,000 a month, so your runway is 6 months.

What this calculator doesn't do

  • It doesn't work out taxes, benefit eligibility or benefit amounts. Enter the money you actually receive.
  • It assumes your expenses and income stay the same every month. Re-run it when something changes.
  • It ignores interest, inflation and one-off costs. Keep a buffer for surprises.

Not financial advice. This is a planning aid that does arithmetic on your numbers.
