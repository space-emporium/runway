      Tips for trimming without misery

      • Pause, don't cancel. Many subscriptions and memberships can be frozen for a few months.
      • Call your providers. Phone, internet and insurance companies often have cheaper plans or hardship options if you ask.
      • Go after the big ones first. One large flexible cost usually buys more time than five small ones.
