A simple plan for the first week

  1. Know your number. Use the runway calculator to see how many months you have today.
  2. Buy yourself time. Put your expenses in the budget trimmer and pause the flexible ones that buy the most runway.
  3. Stay organised. Log every application in the job tracker and set a next step for each one.

Runway does simple arithmetic on the numbers you enter. It doesn't estimate taxes or benefit amounts. Check those with the official source where you live.
