Keeping a job search moving

  • Always set a next step. A follow-up date turns a list into a plan.
  • Follow up after about a week if you haven't heard back. A short, friendly note is enough.
  • Back up regularly. Your data lives only in this browser, so export a CSV now and then (it opens in Excel, Google Sheets or Numbers).
