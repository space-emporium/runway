Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Runway (“we”, “us”, the website at space-emporium.github.io/runway) handles and how. The short version: the numbers and job applications you enter never leave your device. Our advertising partners may use cookies, as described below.

1. Information you enter into the tools

The Savings Runway Calculator, Budget Trimmer and Job Search Tracker run entirely in your web browser using JavaScript. What you type (savings, expenses, income, job applications, notes) is saved in your browser's local storage on your own device so it's still there next time you visit.

  • This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. We don't operate a server or database that could receive it.
  • We can't see, recover or delete it for you. You control it.
  • To delete it, use the “Clear” / “Delete all” buttons in each tool, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • CSV exports are created on your device and saved wherever you choose.

2. Information collected automatically

Like nearly all websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information when your browser requests a page: IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. They use it to deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't use it to identify you.

3. Advertising and cookies

Runway is free because it shows ads. We use Google AdSense, a third-party advertising service from Google. Here is how it works:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info/choices (US) or www.youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services and how Google uses cookies in advertising.

Ad cookies are set by Google and its partners, not by us. We never share the numbers or applications you enter with advertisers, because we don't have them.

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we'll ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown, through a consent message provided by Google. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy/cookie settings link shown on the site.

4. Your choices

  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The tools still work without advertising cookies.
  • Turn off personalised ads via Google Ads Settings (link above). You'll still see ads, but they'll be less tailored.
  • Delete your tool data at any time with the buttons in each tool or by clearing site data.

5. Children

Runway is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights to access, delete or opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. Since we don't collect the data you enter into the tools, there's usually nothing for us to provide. For advertising data, use the opt-out links above or contact Google directly. You can also contact us (see below).

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? See our contact page.
